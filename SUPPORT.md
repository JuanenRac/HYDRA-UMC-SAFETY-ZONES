The best way to understand how to define and calibrate safety zones is through our official YouTube channel:

## ✉️ Direct Technical Inquiries
For industrial safety consulting, ISO certification support, or specific technical blockers:

## 🐛 Bug Reports
If you found a real error in the safety logic or CAN implementation, please open a **GitHub Issue** in this repository. 
*Please search existing issues before opening a new one.*

## ❌ What is NOT support?
- We do not provide support for non-HYDRA-UMC robotic architectures without consulting.
- Please do not use Issues for "How to" questions.
